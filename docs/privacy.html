Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/10/2026

This extension is a new tab page that organizes your bookmarks into categories. It has no account, no server, and no analytics.

What We Store

Your bookmarks, categories and settings are saved with Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.sync). Chrome syncs this data across your own signed-in browsers. We never receive it.

What We Collect

Nothing. The developer does not collect, see, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or bookmarks.

Tabs Permission

When you save the current page or use Quick Add, the extension reads the title and address of your open tabs. This happens on your device only, and only when you click.

Third-Party Requests

  • Site icons are loaded from Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons). Google receives the website domain it is asked for an icon of.
  • The Google Apps menu loads app icons from gstatic.com.
  • If you choose a custom icon by pasting an image link, your browser loads that image from the website you chose, so that website can see the request. Images you upload are stored only on your computer.
  • When you submit the search box, your browser goes to Google Search with your query, just as it would from Google's own page.

These requests are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
