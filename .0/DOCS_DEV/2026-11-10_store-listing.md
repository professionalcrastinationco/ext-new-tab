# Chrome Web Store Listing Draft

Name: Dockside: New Tab Page & Bookmarks
Category: Productivity
Short description (under 132): Organize your favorite links into clean categories on every new tab, with your choice of search engine and calm animated backgrounds.

## Long description
Turn every new tab into a calm, fast launchpad.

- Group bookmarks into your own categories, each with an icon
- A quick-links row for the sites you open all day, with a panel of popular sites (AI chat, Google, creative and dev tools) you can pick in one click
- Drag and drop to reorder your quick links and categories
- Use your own icons: paste an image link (it syncs to your other devices) or upload one
- Search the web from the new tab with the search engine you choose in Settings
- A short walkthrough on first use shows you everything it can do
- Two backgrounds: Spotlight Mesh and an animated Night Sky Aurora with slow shooting stars
- Save the current page in one click from the toolbar popup
- Import and export your bookmarks as a file
- Your data stays in Chrome's own sync storage. No accounts, no tracking.

## Permission justifications (Privacy tab)
- storage: saves your bookmarks, categories and settings.
- tabs: reads the title and address of open tabs for Save Current Page and Quick Add.
- New tab override: the extension's main feature is its new tab page.
- Remote code: No. Data use: no personal data collected or sold.
